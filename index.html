Sci-Fi SFX Lab

  • Tap a Sci-Fi, Retro, Movement (whooshes, jumps, dashes) or Impacts (metal, dirt, leaves, hollow, player hit) button. Each tap rolls a fresh random variation of that type. Then tweak the sliders, and tap the waveform or Play to hear it.
  • Metal ring adds struck-material resonance (inharmonic partials for metal, low values for wood/hollow knocks). Off by default.
  • Engine ⟳ sounds are loop-friendly: playback loops (tap Stop to end) and the WAV carries loop points. Turn Loop on in Output for any sound.
  • Save WAV opens the share sheet on iPhone: pick Save to Files or AirDrop. On computers it downloads.
  • Batch ZIP renders N variations of a preset as WAVs plus a params.json, in one ZIP.
  • Library: every sound you generate is kept here. Tap one to recall it, ★ to keep it, ✎ to rename, ⤓ to save its params as JSON, 🗑 to delete.
  • iPhone: if you hear nothing, check the Ring/Silent switch and volume.
  • Install: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. Works offline after the first visit.
  • Shortcuts: Space play, E WAV, B batch, R random, M mutate, S save, Z undo, W wave, 1–0 presets.
